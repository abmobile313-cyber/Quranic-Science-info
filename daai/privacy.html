Deen Saathi — Privacy Policy

App: Deen Saathi by Quranic Science (package info.quranicscience.daai) · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: Deen Saathi has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics. Everything you record in the app (prayer log, recitation, sadaqah, tasks, period tracker, settings) is stored only on your own phone. We do not have a server that receives your data, and we never sell or share it.

1. Data stored only on your device

  • Settings: language, city or GPS coordinates, school of thought (maslak), gender, targets.
  • Your records: prayer attendance, Quran recitation count, azkar/tasbih counts, sadaqah notes, tasks, reminders, Dawat work log.
  • Women's period tracker (optional): only the start and end dates you enter. It never leaves your phone.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this permanently. You can also export a backup file yourself from Settings.

3. Permissions and why

  • Location – to calculate prayer times and Qibla for where you are. Used only while you set it; you can type a city instead.
  • Notifications – prayer, adhan and reminder alerts.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarm) – so prayer and adhan reminders arrive on time.
  • Microphone – only when you press the mic button to ask the assistant by voice.
  • Run at startup – to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (media playback) – to play the spoken reminder or adhan clip.

4. Children

The app is a general religious utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone; all data stays on the device.
