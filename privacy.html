Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on quranicscience.info and why.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email or mobile number, and the login method you choose (Google, email or phone).
  • Profile: mobile number with country code, city, batch, student code, and, if you add them, your photo and signature for your certificate.
  • Learning: classes opened, attendance, answers to class questions, test results, certificates and questions you send to teachers.
  • Orders and enquiries: what you booked or ordered, and your delivery address when you buy a product.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes your payment. We receive only the payment status, amount, course and the contact details you entered. We never see your card, UPI PIN or bank password.
  • Technical: basic device and usage data used to keep the site secure (Google reCAPTCHA / Firebase App Check) and to count visits (Firebase Analytics).

How we use it

  • To run your account, open your classes after payment, mark attendance and issue certificates.
  • To send you class reminders, payment confirmations and certificate notices by app notification or WhatsApp.
  • To reply to your enquiries and deliver your orders.
  • We never sell your data and never use it for outside advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase. Automation that opens classes and sends reminders runs on Cloudflare. WhatsApp messages are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. These providers process data only on our behalf.

Who can see it

Only you, the owner of Quranic Science, and the teacher of your own course and batch (for attendance and questions). Certificates can be verified publicly by their code, showing your name, course and date.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you logged in and remember your settings (theme, sound, language). The translate feature sets a language cookie.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in My account. To get a copy of your data or delete your account and data, message us on WhatsApp +91 92725 27913 or email quranicscience01@gmail.com; we act within 7 days. Payment records may be kept as required by law.

Children

Students under 18 should use the website with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
