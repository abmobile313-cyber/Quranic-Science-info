About Quranic Science

Ilm Jo Soch Badal De: knowledge that changes the way you think.

Quranic Science began in Mumbai, India, as a channel on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube that explains the Quran's reflections on the natural world in simple Urdu and Hindi. Every video is built on three things: the verse with its classical meaning, peer-reviewed science, and an honest note on how far the link really goes.

What we do

  • Videos and research: short videos with a free research PDF in English, Urdu and Arabic, listing every source.
  • Quranic Science Academy: live and recorded courses in Tajweed, spoken Arabic, AI, video editing and content creation, with a free demo class before you pay.
  • Your AI: a helper on the website that answers questions about our courses and videos in Urdu, Hindi, English and Arabic.
  • Our brands: Flow Studio (video, design and social media), Al Aswad Dubai and ScentKart (fragrances) and Bareerah Naturals (Sunnah foods) support this work.

The founder

Arsalan Ahmed Shaikh is a creator and educator from Mumbai and the founder of Quranic Science, Flow Studio, Al Aswad Dubai and ScentKart. He writes and presents the videos, leads the academy and approves every teacher personally.

Our promise

  • We never present a reflection as 'scientific proof'.
  • We correct mistakes openly: send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we will fix it.
  • We do not issue fatwas; for religious rulings please ask a qualified scholar you trust.
  • We do not accept Zakat, Sadaqah or Khairat on the website.
