Who is this course for

Beginners who want to speak simple Arabic and start understanding the words of the Quran, salah and duas.

After this course you can

  • Read Arabic with harakat confidently
  • Hold a simple everyday conversation
  • Build basic sentences with nouns and verbs
  • Recognise common words in salah and the Quran

Course details

  • LevelBeginner
  • Duration3 months (12 weeks)
  • FormatLive classes + recordings
  • Enrolment1 – 30 November
  • First class1 December 2026
  • TimingsAnnouncing soon

What you will learn

  1. Arabic letters, sounds and reading with harakat
  2. Greetings, introducing yourself and everyday phrases
  3. Nouns, gender and the definite article
  4. Pronouns, possession and simple sentences
  5. Verbs: past, present and commands in daily life
  6. Numbers, time, family, home and shopping conversations
  7. Quranic vocabulary: the most frequent words of the Quran, salah and duas
  8. Speaking practice in pairs and a final conversation with the teacher
