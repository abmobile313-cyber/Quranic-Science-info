Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This policy explains what information Bareerah Naturals (operated by Arsalan Ahmed Shaikh) collects when you use https://quranicscience.info/bareerah/, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email and delivery address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Order details: products, amount and payment status.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, saved in your own browser (for example your cart).

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details go directly to Razorpay and are never stored on our servers.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and track your order.
  • To send order updates by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only what is needed with our payment partner (Razorpay) and courier partners to deliver your order, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time by emailing bareerahnaturals@gmail.com.

Security

Our website uses HTTPS, and we keep your information only as long as needed for orders, accounts and legal records.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
