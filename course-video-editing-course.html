Who is this course for

Beginners and self-taught editors who want to edit reels, shorts and YouTube videos that people actually finish watching.

After this course you can

  • Edit a reel or short from raw footage to upload
  • Add clean captions and good sound
  • Grade colour for a professional look
  • Export correctly for every platform

Course details

  • LevelBeginner to Advanced

What you will learn

  1. Editing basics: timeline, cuts, project settings and a clean folder system
  2. Story and pacing: hooks, jump cuts, b-roll and keeping attention
  3. Captions and text: readable subtitles, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and English text
  4. Colour: correction, grading and a consistent look
  5. Sound design: clean voice, music, ducking and sound effects
  6. Motion basics: zooms, transitions, titles and simple animation
  7. Formats and export: reels, shorts, YouTube, thumbnails and the right settings
  8. Final project: a complete edited video reviewed by the teacher
